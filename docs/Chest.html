Class: Chest

Chest(column, row)

The chest. It opens when the character has a key and stands near it, which costs the key. After the opening animation a portal appears next to it that leads to the boss part of the level.

Constructor

new Chest(column, row)

Creates a chest standing on the bottom edge of a map cell, centered in it, and starts its game loop.
