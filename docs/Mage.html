Class: Mage

Mage(column, row)

The mage, a ranged enemy. It patrols until it sees the character, then it stands still, looks at the character and shoots a fireball whenever its waiting time is over. It patrols again after it lost sight.

Constructor

new Mage(column, row)

Creates a mage on a map cell, looking in a random direction, and starts its game loop.
