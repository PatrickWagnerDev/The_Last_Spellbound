Class: PortalTransition

PortalTransition(world, centerX, centerY, startSize, levelData)

The transition into the boss part: a spiral circle grows from the portal until it covers the screen, the new level is loaded, and the circle shrinks around the character. It keeps running during a pause.

Constructor

new PortalTransition(world, centerX, centerY, startSize, levelData)

Creates the transition at the portal, starts its game loop and plays the portal sound.
Parameters:
Name Type Description
world World The world that runs the transition.
centerX number Horizontal center of the circle on the screen.
centerY number Vertical center of the circle on the screen.
startSize number Size of the portal the circle grows from.
levelData object Data of the level part that is loaded in the middle of the transition.
