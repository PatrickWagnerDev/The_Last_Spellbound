Class: SoundManager

SoundManager()

The one place for all sounds of the game: short sound effects, the looping music, looping sounds such as steps, and sounds that differ by character. It also remembers whether the sound is muted.

Constructor

new SoundManager()

Creates the sound manager: reads the saved mute setting, prepares the storages and loads the sound effects.
