Class: Enemy

Enemy(column, row, config)

Base class of all enemies and bosses. It builds an enemy from its config and runs the common tick: falling, death, hurt phase, gravity and then the behavior of the subclass. It also handles hits with knockback, turning at edges, the hurt boxes that touch the character, and the removal after death.
