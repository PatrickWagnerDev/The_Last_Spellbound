Class: Boss

Boss(column, row, config)

Base class of the bosses. It runs a state machine: wait or patrol until the boss sees the character, then sneer, chase and attack, and search and patrol again when the character is lost. Subclasses add their own states and attacks. The first time the boss notices the character, the boss music starts.
