Class: CharacterActions

CharacterActions()

Actions of the character: the roll, the sword attack and the fireball spell with their durations, waiting times and keys, and the energy the fireball costs. It also changes some methods of the movement so that rolling, being hurt and acting affect it. Character builds on it.
