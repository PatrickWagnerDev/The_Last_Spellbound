Class: KnightAttacks

KnightAttacks()

The attacks of the dark knight, on top of the sprint and the jump over gaps of the jump boss: a thrust standing and one while walking, a slash upwards and an aura with a long wind-up. Each attack has its own waiting time. The dark knight builds on it.
