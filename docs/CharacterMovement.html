Class: CharacterMovement

CharacterMovement()

Movement of the character: walking with the keys, jumping with crouch, flight and landing, the short extra time to jump after leaving a platform, and counting the time without input. It is the lowest of the three classes that make up the character, the actions (CharacterActions) and the whole character (Character) build on it.
