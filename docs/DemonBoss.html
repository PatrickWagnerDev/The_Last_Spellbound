Class: DemonBoss

DemonBoss(column, row)

The demon boss of level two. On top of the sprint and the jump over gaps of the jump boss it fights with a club: when the character is within reach it swings, the club only hits during a short part of the swing, and then the demon has to wait before it can swing again.

Constructor

new DemonBoss(column, row)

Creates the demon on a map cell, looking to the left, and starts its game loop. It starts on patrol.
