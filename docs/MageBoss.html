Class: MageBoss

MageBoss(column, row)

The mage boss of level one. On top of the boss behavior it has two ranged attacks: a fireball, shot low or high depending on the height of the character, and a long ray that only hits targets near the ground. It attacks only when it sees the character and prefers the ray when it is ready.
