Class: Bear

Bear(column, row)

The bear, the simplest enemy: it does not see the character and only walks back and forth on its platform. It hurts the character by touching it.

Constructor

new Bear(column, row)

Creates a bear on a map cell, looking in a random direction, and starts its game loop.
