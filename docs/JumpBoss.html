Class: JumpBoss

JumpBoss(column, row, config)

Base class of the demon and, through the knight attacks, of the dark knight. On top of the boss behavior it adds the sprint, which makes the boss faster for a while, and the jump over gaps, and it stops at edges it cannot jump over.
