Markers

Enter the number in the map where the figure stands. It stands with its feet on the bottom edge of that cell. Numbers 60 to 68 are items (60 is the key, 61 is the pizza, 62 is the donut, 63 is the chest), number 69 is the start of the player, normal enemies use the numbers 70 to 89, boss enemies use the numbers 90 to 99 (90 is the mage boss, 91 is the demon boss, 92 is the dark knight).

60 Key
61 Pizza (heals 1 HP)
62 Donut (refills the energy)
63 Chest (opens with a key)
90 Mage boss
91 Demon boss
92 Dark knight
69 Player start
70 Bear
71 Mage (shoots fireballs)
